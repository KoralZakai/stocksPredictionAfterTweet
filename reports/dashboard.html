THE HYPOTHESIS: a geopolitical shock ("Iran", "Hormuz", "strike") should move oil and the fear gauge within the hour. Daily bars are too slow to see it — go intraday and the "Heavy Hitters" will separate from the noise.

Design

Within-event control: each tweet's own 60-minute PRE-window is its control, which cancels the market-hours selection bias that killed our earlier 1h result. Cohort control: NOISE posts on the same asset — "GEO moves oil" only counts if it moves oil more than a random in-session post does. Excess is measured vs SPY on public yfinance hourly bars (no private feed), σ is backward-only, and the registry was written before scoring.

THE NEAR-MISS — the most dangerous artifact we caught. The first run reported 9 cells surviving BH at p=0.0018: "GEO tweets spike oil volume 1.79× vs 1.41×". It was entirely duplicate counting. He posts in bursts, and every tweet inside one hour resolves to the same hourly bar — two posts a minute apart both scored gold at z=−11.16. Collapsing to one observation per (asset, bar) took 10,245 rows down to 5,552 and the survivors from 9 → 0. We found it only because the Heavy Hitter list looked absurd.
